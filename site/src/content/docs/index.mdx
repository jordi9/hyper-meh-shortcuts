## How it works

**Caps Lock** is remapped via Karabiner-Elements into two modifier layers:

| Layer | Activation | Modifier combo |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **Meh** | Caps Lock held | `Ctrl + Shift + Option` |
| **Hyper** | Caps Lock + Cmd held | `Ctrl + Shift + Option + Cmd` |

Tapping Caps Lock alone sends **F13**.
